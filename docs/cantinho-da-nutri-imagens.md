## Prompts usados

### Kit da vida real
Editorial food photograph for a Brazilian nutrition website card. A reusable shopping bag on a kitchen counter with plain unbranded yogurt pots, bottled protein drink, jar of mixed nuts, oat snack bars and fruit. Practical packaged supermarket snacks. Warm natural daylight, realistic appetizing materials, muted warm palette, horizontal 3:2 composition. No readable text, no logos, no typography.

### Refeições práticas
Editorial food photograph for a nutrition website card. Overhead view on a warm stone kitchen surface of a simple balanced breakfast bowl with yogurt oats banana, a lunch plate with rice beans vegetables chicken, and a dinner plate with vegetables and fish. Everyday Brazilian meals, accessible portions, natural light, realistic food photography, horizontal 3:2 composition. No text, no logos.

### Produtos do dia a dia
Editorial photograph for a nutrition website card. Close-up hands comparing nutrition label panels on two plain unbranded grocery packages next to yogurt and oats on a supermarket shelf, neutral colors, shallow depth of field, natural realistic lighting, horizontal 3:2 composition. Label details may be blurred and must have no legible claims or brand names. No added text.

### SOS da vida real
Editorial food photograph for a Brazilian nutrition website card. Friends sharing a relaxed restaurant meal, only hands and table visible, varied plates of salad, grilled vegetables, burger and a few pizza slices. Friendly social moment and conscious choices, natural warm lighting, realistic food photography, horizontal 3:2 composition. No text, no logos.

### Guia de hidratação e sono
Editorial lifestyle photograph for a hydration and sleep guide website card. Quiet cozy bedroom in morning sunlight, linen bedding, a bedside wooden table with a clear glass of water and a reusable glass water bottle, warm beige and soft green natural palette, peaceful atmosphere, realistic photography, horizontal 3:2 composition. No people, no text, no logos.
